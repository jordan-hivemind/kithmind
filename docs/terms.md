# Terms of use and data disclosure

Kith Mind is open-source software (MIT license) that a person runs for their
own household. There is no hosted service operated for the public. These terms
describe how a self-hosted instance handles the data it is authorized to read.

## What the software does with your data

- It reads records you authorize (financial institution feeds, health records
  from a patient portal, and documents in a folder you choose) and stores them
  in a database that you own and control.
- It uses the records to answer your own questions and to show you summaries in
  your own instance. Model providers you configure may process excerpts to
  produce those answers, under their own terms.
- It does not sell, share, advertise with, or transmit your records to anyone
  else. Nothing is sent to the software's authors.

## Health records

- Access to a patient portal is authorized by you, for you and for any family
  member whose proxy access you hold, and can be revoked at any time in the
  portal.
- Records are stored only in your database. You can delete them at any time by
  removing the instance's data.

## Your responsibilities

- You are responsible for securing the machine and database that run your
  instance, including backups.
- The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, as stated in
  the MIT license.
